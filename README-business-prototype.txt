Marketly business prototype

Open business-register.html first.

Prototype flow:
1. Register a business.
2. Choose monthly or yearly subscription in Indian rupees.
3. Enter demo card details. No payment is processed; it is a front-end prototype.
4. After registration, you are taken to business-home.html.
5. Use business-login.html to log in again with the same email and password.
6. Use business-listing.html to fill sector-tailored fields.

Sector-tailored fields included for:
- Hotel / Accommodation
- Restaurant / Cafe
- Clothing / Retail
- Health & Beauty / Nail Art
- Freelancer
- Service Provider
- Events / Venue
- Real Estate
- Other

Important: Real card payment should be implemented using a secure payment gateway such as Razorpay, Stripe India, PayU or Cashfree. Never store raw card numbers in your own database.
