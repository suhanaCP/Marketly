Popular questions

Quick answers to what people ask us most.

How do I create a Marketly account?

Select "Sign in" in the top menu, then "Create an account" on the sign-in page. You'll need an email address and a password. No payment details are required for a customer account.

How do I contact or book a business?

Open any listing from Explore or Categories to see contact details, booking links and opening hours provided directly by the business. Marketly connects you to the business; availability and confirmation are handled by them.

How do I list my business on Marketly?

Choose "List your business" from the menu, fill in your business and sector details, then pick a subscription plan. Your profile is reviewed before it goes live.

Is Marketly free to use for customers?

Yes. Browsing, searching and contacting businesses is always free for customers. Only business owners pay a subscription to list and manage a profile.

How do reviews work?

Reviews are left by customers after an interaction with a business. We look out for unusual patterns to keep ratings genuine, and businesses can publicly respond to reviews on their profile.

I forgot my password, what do I do?

On the sign-in page, use the link below the form to reset your password. You'll get an email with instructions to set a new one.

What subscription plans are available for businesses?

We offer Starter, Growth and Premium plans, billed monthly or yearly, based on business size and features needed. You can compare and choose a plan during business registration.

How do I cancel my business subscription?

Go to your business dashboard settings and select "Manage subscription." Your listing stays active until the end of the current billing period.
